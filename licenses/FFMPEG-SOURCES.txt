FFmpeg / FFprobe downloaded by the optional macOS installer

Version: FFmpeg 9.0.2, independent precompiled builds by Martin Riedl.
The FastTracker ZIP does not include the FFmpeg/FFprobe binaries. Installation
downloads the unmodified archives from the builder and verifies pinned SHA-256.

including libx264/libx265. Treat the resulting FFmpeg programs as GPLv3+.
